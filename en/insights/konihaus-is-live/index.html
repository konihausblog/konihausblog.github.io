Konihaus Is Live

October 15, 2026   ·   4 min read   ·   by Koni
Share

Today the Idea Becomes a Service

In recent weeks we have written about why a smart home should be personal, private, and built to last. Today those ideas stop being ideas: Konihaus is live at konihaus.ch.

What Konihaus Is: Smart Home Service in Switzerland

Konihaus is a smart home with a real person behind it. I plan, set up, and look after your system, and I stay with you afterwards. Everything runs locally on a hub in your home, built on open-source Home Assistant, with Zigbee, Matter, and Thread devices from different manufacturers working together. There is no mandatory cloud and no single platform you are tied to. Remote access, if you want it, goes through an encrypted private VPN.

Who It Is For

For seniors who want safety at night and independence by day. For tenants who cannot drill into walls. For owners of holiday homes who want to be present without being there. You can see how this looks in practice in our examples, or read Who Is Konihaus For?.

How It Works

  1. Free consultation. 30 minutes, online or on site, without a sales pitch.
  2. Installation and setup. On site in 2 to 4 hours, with no intervention in the walls.
  3. Introduction. A walkthrough for your household in German or English, with a handbook.
  4. Ongoing care. Remote monitoring, updates, and support are included, with a monthly report.

You can find the full process at konihaus.ch.

What It Costs

Every package includes installation, setup, and ongoing remote support:

  • Basis, from CHF 39 per month: a 1 to 2 room apartment, about 7 devices.
  • Standard, from CHF 79 per month: a 3-room apartment, about 8 devices.
  • Advanced, from CHF 139 per month: a single-family house, about 12-15 devices.
  • All-in, from CHF 249 per month: a large house, 20+ devices with individual automations.

These are guide values including VAT. For context, see what a smart home costs in Switzerland. The hardware is a one-time cost at the first installation, and your final quote depends on your home and the automations you want. All details are on the packages page.

Four Things We Stand For

  • Personal. One person stays with you, before and after the installation. Read more
  • Private. Your home's data stays on hardware in your home. Read more
  • Open. Combine devices from different manufacturers without being locked in. Read more
  • Swiss. Established, maintained, and operated in Switzerland, and made to last. Read more

One Honest Note

Konihaus is not an emergency service. For emergencies, always call the usual emergency numbers. For everything else, we answer within 24 hours.
